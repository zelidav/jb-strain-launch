Cherry Gushers

Genetics — Barney's Farm × Backpack Boyz

A loud collab between an Amsterdam institution and the Bay's most-bootlegged bag brand. Black Cherry Punch crossed into Gushers gives you ripe-cherry candy on the inhale and a heavy-blanket finish — the kind of jar that makes the room go quiet, then funny, then horizontal. Indica-leaning hybrid, dense, gas-forward, no notes.

Haze

Genetics — Greenhouse Seed Co.

The mother of every modern sativa worth lighting. Greenhouse keeps the original Haze line alive out of Amsterdam — Mexican, Colombian, Thai, and South Indian landraces stitched together into one electric, incense-and-citrus sativa. Spicy on the pull, cerebral by the second hit, and creative for hours. This is the one you smoke when the work matters.

LineageMex × Col × Thai × S. Indian
THC Range18–22%
OriginAmsterdam, est. 1994
ProfileSpice · Citrus · Incense
TerpinolenePineneOcimene

Double Runtz

Genetics — 7ACRES

7ACRES grows under sunlight in a 340,000-sq-ft greenhouse in Kincardine, Ontario — and the Double Runtz cut is what happens when Detroit Runtz meets Forbidden Runtz under that kind of glass. Gas, fruit, candy, grape — the four food groups. Balanced enough to talk through, heavy enough to ride out. A genuine flagship hybrid for the lineup.

Pink grapefruit on the nose, diesel under the floor, and a sativa lift that feels like a 6 train pulling out of Grand Central. Talkative, social, daytime — the bag you pass at the cookout.

Super Boof

Genetics — Blockhead (San Diego)

Leafly's 2024 Strain of the Year. Bred by Blockhead in San Diego as "Blockberry" — Black Cherry Punch into Tropicanna Cookies — then renamed and rocketed into the canon. Berry, citrus, funk, all on top of dense purple buds running 28-32% THC. Indica-leaning hybrid that giggles before it drops you. The cult joint with the receipts.

About

JBD. Since '91.

Jerome Baker Designs has been blowing borosilicate since 1991 — a Pacific Northwest glass studio that put hand-built American glass on the cultural map and never let it slip. The pieces in NY Drop 1 carry hand-painted drop patterns, color-coded by strain, and every collectible piece is provenance-verified through the JBD Glass app.

The flower lineup follows the same standard. Every strain in this drop comes from a breeder who put their name on it before it became a trend — Barney's Farm, Greenhouse, 7ACRES, Exotic Genetix, Humboldt Seed Co., Blockhead, Cannarado. Genetics with receipts. Glass with receipts.
